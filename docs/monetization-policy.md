# Monetization policy

Status: active  
Last updated: 2026-11-25

This policy covers official Rune Launcher and RuneModLoader / RuneCore
distributions. Third-party mods are independent works and are not covered by
this policy.

## Commitment

Rune Launcher and RuneModLoader are free, non-commercial projects.

- No paid versions, paid tiers, or paid licenses.
- No paid early access, paid beta, or donor-only builds.
- No paid support, priority support, or paid feature requests.
- No advertising, sponsorships, affiliate links, or revenue sharing tied to the
  launcher or loader.
- No resale of the launcher, loader, or bundled assets.
- No commercial mod hosting or paid mod distribution operated by this project.

## Donations

Voluntary donations are acceptable only when they do not purchase anything.

- Donations must not unlock builds, features, priority support, or early access.
- Donations must not be required to use the launcher or loader.
- Donation pages must not imply that a payment buys a license, ownership, or
  special status.
- Donation rewards, commissions, or paid requests are not offered.
- Donation platforms and their terms must be reviewed separately.

Suggested wording:

```text
Donations are voluntary and do not purchase any product, license, feature, or
priority access. Rune Launcher and RuneModLoader are free, unofficial projects.
```

## Distribution

- Public launcher releases are launcher-only and do not bundle RuneCore.
- RuneModLoader / RuneCore remains a private beta unless explicitly announced.
- No distribution through Microsoft Store, Xbox, Realms, or console platforms
  without explicit permission.
- No modified game files, game assets, or proprietary binaries are distributed.

## Compliance boundaries

The project does not provide, and must not be presented as providing:

- authentication, DRM, or anti-cheat bypasses;
- cheats or unfair advantages on public servers;
- modified or redistributed Minecraft game files;
- official endorsement by Mojang Studios or Microsoft.

Contributors and distributors should review the Minecraft EULA, Usage
Guidelines, and Commercial Guidelines, and seek independent legal advice for
their own circumstances. This document is a project policy, not legal advice.

## Pause and removal

If Mojang, Microsoft, or a platform provider requests a change or removal, the
affected distribution will be paused or removed while the request is reviewed.
The launcher-only build is designed to remain available independently of the
private loader.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project or platform requirements change.
Material changes should be recorded in the repository history.
